Thumbs up/down plus optional structured reasons (wrong incident, wrong resolution, incomplete,

============================================================
SAFETY / GUARDRAILS
============================================================

Never generate destructive/dangerous action recommendations without strong grounding. Require
retrieval grounding for recommendations, flag unsupported ones, prefer recommendations backed by
multiple historical sources, avoid destructive commands, keep historical evidence visibly separate
from LLM inference (via the Evidence Chain's observed/derived/inferred tagging), escalate
uncertain/high-risk cases rather than guessing, never invent historical incidents or resolutions
that don't exist in the (real or clearly-flagged-synthetic) dataset. The system is a decision-
support tool, not an autonomous production administrator — destructive actions always require
human confirmation.
